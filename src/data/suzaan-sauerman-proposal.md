## 1. What we’re building

We’ll build your content around your experience with wearables, women’s health and AI. On Instagram and TikTok, you’ll help people make more informed choices about health technology. On LinkedIn, we’ll develop your perspective for product teams and event organisers and give them a clear way to contact you about advisory work, keynotes and panel discussions.

Take your 11 years of health tracking across four wearables. In a Reel, you could show what you still have to piece together by hand. A carousel could map where your data ends up. On LinkedIn, we could turn that experience into a question for product teams: **what would it take to give people a clear picture of their health without a spreadsheet?**

## 5. How we’ll work together

1. **Strategy week**\
   I review your content and prepare the initial direction. We agree on your first filming date or footage deadline.
2. **Strategy discussion**\
   We review the proposed formats and topics together and decide what to start with.
3. **First batch and approval**\
   You supply footage or book a shoot. I prepare the first posts and send them to you for approval.
4. **Three months of publishing**\
   The first month starts when the first approved post goes live. We use each monthly review to adjust what we publish next.
5. **End-of-term review**\
   We decide which formats to continue, revise, or drop, and whether to adjust the publishing cadence or scope.

### Payment, approvals and handover

- Social media payment — Strategy setup and the first publishing month are invoiced before work begins. The second and third months, and any continuation, are invoiced in advance.
- Website payment and timeline — 50% at booking and 50% after final approval. We agree on the schedule before work begins, based on platform access and delivery of materials. After final payment, I hand over the site and relevant access. Ongoing maintenance can be agreed separately.
- Content responsibilities — You supply account access, source material, usable footage and imagery, and approve factual and health-related claims and third-party material. You handle replies and conversations with prospective clients and event organisers.
